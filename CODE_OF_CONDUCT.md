# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, safe, and respectful environment
for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate constructively and respectfully.
- Welcome questions and differing technical perspectives.
- Focus feedback on the work, not the person.
- Protect private information and credentials.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and publication
of another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainer. Reports will
be reviewed fairly and confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove content,
reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation
when necessary to protect the community.
